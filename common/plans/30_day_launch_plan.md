# SPICO Vita – 30-Day Launch Action Plan (13 Oct – 12 Nov 2026)
Owner: Tinku (solo). Budget ₹1,00,000. All ₹ figures are estimates from team notes, not quotes.

## Strategy in one line
Launch small and local: 2 SKUs (toor + moong, 500 g & 1 kg) sold to kiranas and line suppliers in BTM-adjacent Koramangala/JP Nagar/Jayanagar first; quick-commerce only once real repacker costs show a viable MRP.

## Gate decisions for Tinku (needed in week 1)
1. ON HOLD (13 Oct, 5:30 PM): product list being reconsidered by Tinku. Plan currently drafted on 2 SKUs (toor + moong); revise once final list is confirmed.
2. Approve marketing cap ₹8,000.
3. Brand: wait for 'SPICO' class-30 trademark search; if conflict, choose fallback name before printing packs.
4. Intro trade offer level for kiranas (Accounts: up to 5% off or 1 free per 10).
5. Regular GST vs composition (Accounts recommends regular; confirm with a CA).
6. Pick 2–3 fallback brand names now so they go into the same free trademark search.

## Week 1 (13–19 Oct): Compliance & foundations
- Run free trademark search (SPICO / SPICOVITA / SPICO VITA, classes 29/30/31). No pack printing until clear.
- Call FoSCoS 1800112100; add Marketer/repacker kind of business to FSSAI 21226194003349.
- Apply BBMP trade licence for BTM premises (~₹1–5k; budget ₹3,000).
- Open current account (needs GST cert when issued); install Vyapar or myBillBook.
- Get 3 supplier quotes (toor, moong) + buy samples (~₹2,000).
- Get 2–3 repacker quotes (per-kg rate, min order, FSSAI licence of repacker).
- KPI: 3 supplier + 2 repacker quotes in hand; licence applications filed.

## Week 2 (20–26 Oct): Product & pricing locked
- Pick supplier and repacker; place first trial order (~₹30k stock, 2 SKUs).
- Send samples for NABL test (~₹3k per SKU, ₹6k total) – needed for nutrition panel.
- Cost Controller finalises landed cost/kg; Accounts sets kirana price (floor ~₹155/kg on toor at ₹142 landed).
- Finalise pack artwork only if trademark is clear; print small run.
- Free setup: Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business catalogue, Instagram.
- KPI: landed cost confirmed; pack printed or on order.

## Week 3 (27 Oct – 2 Nov): First sales
- Stock arrives; repack; label check against FSSAI rules (FSSAI no., batch, MFD/best before, net qty, MRP).
- Visit 40 kiranas (≈10/day over 4 days) in Koramangala, BTM, JP Nagar, Jayanagar with samples + flyer.
- Meet 3–5 line suppliers serving those areas.
- KPI: 10 kiranas stocking; 1 line supplier signed.

## Week 4 (3–12 Nov): Repeat & review
- Re-visit stockists for reorder; collect feedback on price/quality.
- Apartment WhatsApp groups (Marketing plan) for direct household orders.
- Prepare quick-commerce file (FSSAI, GST, lab report, images); ask Blinkit about non-GS1 code.
- Day-30 review: sales, cash left, reorder rate. Decide whether to add urad/chana.
- KPI: ₹25k+ sales, 50%+ stockists reorder, cash buffer ≥ ₹8k.

## Budget view (from Cost Controller, 2-SKU case)
Stock ₹30k | Pouches/labels ₹12k | Repack+transport ₹5k | Branding ₹7k | Trademark ₹4.5k | Lab ₹6k | Onboarding/photos ₹2k | Marketing ₹8k | BBMP ₹3k | Samples ₹2k = ~₹79.5k; buffer ~₹20k (4-SKU case leaves ~₹0–2k).

## Stop/rethink triggers
- Trademark conflict confirmed → rename before any print.
- Landed cost > ₹150/kg toor → renegotiate or switch supplier.
- Fewer than 5 stockists by Day 21 → change area or offer.
