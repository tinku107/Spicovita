# Spicovita – Line suppliers & quick-commerce onboarding (research note, 13-Oct-2026)

## Finding Bangalore line suppliers / distributors for kirana shops
- Line suppliers buy from APMC Yeshwanthpur traders and run fixed weekly routes to kiranas. Ask the APMC traders you buy from (Sanghavi, ATM Pulses, Sri R K Traders) who carries their goods to South Bangalore – that is the fastest lead.
- Visit 10–15 kiranas in Koramangala/Jayanagar/JP Nagar/E-City and ask "who supplies your dal/FMCG?" – note name + phone printed on their bills.
- Bangalore Wholesale Food Grains & Pulses Merchants Association (APMC) – member lists.
- Search IndiaMART/JustDial: "FMCG distributor Jayanagar", "grocery line supplier Electronic City"; also Udaan / Jumbotail (B2B apps supplying kiranas in Bangalore) as a channel.
- Pitch: rate card with distributor margin (typically 8–12%) + retailer margin (typically 10–15%) under MRP, credit terms, samples. [margins = assumption; verify locally]

Common docs: GSTIN (needed – wait for approval), PAN, current bank account + cancelled cheque, FSSAI licence, trademark application/brand proof, GS1 EAN barcodes per SKU, Legal-Metrology-compliant label (MRP, net qty, mfg/packer address, FSSAI no., best-before), product photos, shelf life, often a lab test report. APOB in GST needed for each state warehouse. Onboarding typically 2–6 weeks.
