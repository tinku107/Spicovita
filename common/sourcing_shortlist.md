## D. Blinkit seller onboarding checklist
Portal: https://seller.blinkit.com (self-serve Seller Hub; document check takes 12–48 h per Blinkit).
- **Business/brand:** brand info, manufacturer details, trademark certificate or TM-A application, logo (576×576 PNG/JPG), and an authorisation letter if you aren't the brand owner. (Blinkit FAQ; arvian.in)
- **Tax & bank:** GSTIN (Regular, not Composition), PAN, current account and cancelled cheque. The name must match across GST, PAN, bank and FSSAI. You also need APOB on GST for the dark-store/warehouse address, plus IRN (e-invoice) credentials and a digital signature. (Blinkit FAQ; confetti.design; base.com)
- **FSSAI:** the licence needs ≥3 months validity left, and the number must be printed on the primary pack (stickers or inserts are rejected at inward checks per base.com).
  - ⚠ **Basic Registration (Retailer) is most likely NOT enough.** FSSAI kinds of business: if you pack bulk dal into your own packs you're a **Repacker**, and if a co-packer packs under your brand you're a **Relabeller**. Both are manufacturer categories, and "Marketer" was disabled in 2021. FSSAI's e-commerce rule says sellers/brand owners on platforms must display their licence/registration, and the guidance documents mention a Central licence for e-commerce FBOs. Under the FoSCoS eligibility table (rev. 01.04.2026), **pulses *milling* units need a State licence at any turnover**, while repacking under ₹1.5 Cr can still be Registration. Recommended: modify or apply for the Relabeller/Repacker kind of business, and ask FoSCoS (1800112100) whether a State licence is needed. stryderconsulting also notes that a Central licence avoids gaps for multi-state supply. Sources: https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/officer/assets/docs/Revised_2ndApril2026KindofBusinessEligibility.pdf, https://foodraksha.com/fssai-license-for-repacking/, https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/L-and-R%20oper%20content_merged.pdf
- **Labelling (Legal Metrology + FSSAI):** product name, net qty, MRP incl. taxes, mfg/pack date, best before, batch, "Packed by"/"Marketed by" name and address, FSSAI logo and number, veg mark, country of origin, consumer care contact. Your MRP must be the same across all channels.
- **Catalogue (NPI template):** titles, ≥3 images per SKU, MRP, supply price, EAN, net qty, dimensions, HSN, shelf life (Blinkit wants ~90–120 days remaining at dispatch per confetti.design).
- **EAN/GS1 barcode:** every SKU and pack size needs a unique GS1 EAN-13. Several 2026 guides say **self-generated or non-GS1 codes are rejected** (arvian.in, confetti.design). GS1 India costs about ₹50,200 for 100 codes (see licences_note.md).
  - **Barcode exception / internal code:** I found **no official Blinkit documentation of a barcode exception process**. The only route is to ask through Seller Hub support or the assigned category manager after you register: "We're a new brand awaiting GS1 allocation; can SKUs be onboarded with a Blinkit-generated internal/SKU code temporarily?" Expect a no. Treat this as unconfirmed. Plan on GS1 (budget ~₹50k) before Blinkit, or start with kirana and line suppliers first.
- Commission: reported bands of 2/6/13/18% by price band (ET 2025, in quick_commerce note). Timeline 2–6 weeks.
