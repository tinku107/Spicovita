## 1. BBMP Trade Licence (Bengaluru)
- Portal (new application): https://trade.bbmpgov.in/Forms/frmTradeLicenceRegistration.aspx · FAQ: https://site.bbmp.gov.in/tradelicensefaq.html · Process: https://site.bbmp.gov.in/departmentwebsites/BBMPIT/OTLS.html
- Process: accept terms, enter trade details (trade type/name, property, ward, major/minor/sub trade), upload documents, get a temporary application number, then pay online (or at a bank/MoH office). The Medical Officer of Health/DHO approves it. Validity runs 1–5 years by financial year (to 31 Mar). Renew before 28 Feb to avoid a penalty.
- Documents (BBMP FAQ): owner consent letter or lease agreement, electricity bill, and occupancy certificate if available (JPG/PDF, max 1 MB each). Also keep ID and PAN, FSSAI and GST.
- Fees: set by Annexure A schedule (https://dl.bbmpgov.in/download/tradelic/Annexure-A.pdf), from ₹250 to ₹1,00,000 depending on category and area. Food-articles sale/packing (provision stores) and packed foods fall roughly in the ₹1,000–₹5,000 band for small premises. Running more than one trade at the same premises is charged at twice the highest applicable fee. Confirm the exact category on the portal.
- **Virtual office:** A trade licence is issued for a *physical premises where trade is carried on*, and the declaration says it can be cancelled if the premises breaks zoning rules. A virtual office (desk or address only, with no stock or sales there) generally won't qualify for a food-trade licence, and the BBMP health inspector may visit. Use the actual place where stock is stored or sold, such as the BTM/Kadabisanahalli address on the FSSAI certificate. If no stock is kept and you only trade/market, check with the ward MoH whether you need a licence at all. (This is my reading, not an official statement.)
- Note: BBMP was restructured under Greater Bengaluru Authority in 2025; the portal URL above is still the live trade-licence system.

## 2. GS1 India Barcodes
- Apply: https://www.gs1india.org/register-for-barcodes · Fee PDF (effective 1 Oct 2026): https://admin.gs1india.org/uploads/GCP_Fees_e3ed978cac.pdf
- Minimum capacity is 100 barcodes. For turnover up to ₹5 Cr and 1 year: ₹27,000 registration + ₹13,000 subscription + ₹7,200 GST + ₹3,000 refundable deposit = **₹50,200**. 1,000 barcodes cost ₹55,510.
- Only the brand owner can apply. It takes 7–10 working days. Capacity can't be changed later.
- Documents: company/proprietor proof (GST certificate, PAN), cancelled cheque, signed application. A new business does not need a balance sheet. Brand/trademark details help.
- MSME subsidy: 80% reimbursement for *micro-manufacturing* MSMEs (cap ₹50,650), claimed through MSME-DI. https://www.gs1india.org/msme. A pure trader/repacker may not qualify.
- ⚠ This is about half of your ₹1 lakh budget. Hold off until a quick-commerce platform actually requires GTINs. Local kirana/line distribution doesn't need them.

## 3. Trademark – "SPICO Vita"
- Search (free, OTP login): https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch/ (wordmark + phonetic searches for "SPICO", "SPICOVITA", "SPICO VITA" in classes 30 and 31) · AI search: https://tmsearch.ipindia.gov.in/ords/r/tisa/trademark_search600/login
- File: https://ipindiaonline.gov.in/trademarkefiling/ using Form TM-A.
- Fee (https://www.ipindia.gov.in/pages/trade-marks/learn/forms-and-official-fees): **₹4,500 per class (e-filing)** for an individual, startup or small enterprise, and ₹9,000 for others. Classes 30 and 31 together cost ₹9,000.
- Class guide: processed/split dal is usually **Class 30** (flour and cereal preparations) or 29 (dried pulses). Raw, unprocessed grains are Class 31. Check that the goods description in the TM class tool matches "pulses / dal". Many brands file in 29 + 30 + 31.
- Documents: applicant ID/address, logo image (JPG), user affidavit if claiming prior use (otherwise "proposed to be used"), and an Udyam certificate if claiming small-enterprise status. You can use "TM" on packs right after filing; "®" is allowed only after registration.
