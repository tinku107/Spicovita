# SPICO Vita – GST & Margin Rules (draft, Accounts, 13 Oct 2026)
Verify GST points with a CA before first invoice.

## GST
- Pre-packaged, labelled pulses up to 25 kg (our 500 g / 1 kg packs): 5% GST (HSN 0713). Loose/bulk above 25 kg: exempt.
- Supplier bulk bags (50 kg) usually come GST-free, so little input credit on stock. ITC mainly on pouches, printing, repacking job work (typically 18%).
- Until the GSTIN arrives: no tax invoices; record sales as bills of supply and avoid B2B sales that need GST invoices.
- Scheme choice (Tinku + CA): Regular (5% out, claim ITC, needed for Blinkit/Zepto/Instamart B2B) vs Composition (1% of turnover, no ITC, simpler). Recommend Regular, since quick-commerce is the plan.
- Billing: Vyapar or myBillBook, HSN 0713 on every item, 5% rate.

## Margin rules (all prices ex-GST; add 5% on top)
- Cost base: landed cost per kg (Cost Controller's sheet). Toor today: ₹142.
- Floor: never below landed × 1.09 (toor ₹155/kg ex-GST = ₹163 incl.).
- Kirana / line supplier price: landed × 1.12–1.15 (toor ₹159–163 ex-GST, ₹167–171 incl.).
- Kirana retail margin to leave them: 15–20% between our price and MRP. So toor 1 kg MRP ≈ ₹199–210.
- Intro offer: max 5% off or 1 free pack per 10 (stays above floor).
- Quick-commerce: list only if (MRP ex-GST − 18% commission − 5% fees) ≥ landed × 1.12. Toor needs MRP ≈ ₹229; on hold.
- Credit to kiranas: max 7 days, max ₹3,000 outstanding per shop.
- Recompute all prices when real supplier/repacker quotes land.
