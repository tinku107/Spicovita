# SPICO Vita – Brand Basics (draft, 14 Oct)
Name is provisional until the 'SPICO' class 30 trademark check clears.

## Positioning
For Bangalore families who want everyday dal they can trust, SPICO Vita offers clean, carefully sorted pulses packed fresh in the city, with honest weight and simple pricing from your neighbourhood kirana.
Proof points: sorted & cleaned, packed locally, sealed pouch, FSSAI-registered.
Avoid: "organic", "unpolished", "chemical-free", or health claims unless supplier/lab documents prove them.

## Tagline options
1. Clean dal. Honest weight.
2. Everyday dal, done right.
3. Sorted with care, packed in Bangalore.
4. Good dal, every day.

## Front of pack (per SKU)
SPICO Vita | Toor Dal | [tagline] | Net Wt 500 g / 1 kg | Cleaned & sorted

## Back-of-pack copy
"We source [toor/moong] dal from trusted mills, clean and sort it, and pack it fresh in Bangalore so your dal cooks evenly and tastes the way it should."
Cooking tip (toor): Rinse well, soak 20–30 min, pressure cook with 3 cups water per cup of dal for 3–4 whistles.
Storage: Store in a cool, dry place. Transfer to an airtight container after opening.

## Mandatory label items (FSSAI – confirm with repacker/consultant)
Product name, ingredient, net quantity, MRP (incl. taxes), batch/lot no., date of packing, best before, nutrition info (from lab test), veg (green dot) logo, FSSAI logo + licence no. (Marketer category needed), manufacturer/packer and marketer name & address, customer care phone/email.
