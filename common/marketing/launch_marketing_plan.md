# SPICO Vita – Low-Cost Launch Marketing Plan (first 30 days)
Marketing budget cap: ₹8,000 of the ₹1,00,000 (subject to Cost Controller review). No figures below are quotes; verify before spending.

## Positioning (draft – brand choice needs Tinku)
"Clean, sorted, unpolished dal from Bangalore – honest weight, fresh stock."
Products: toor, urad, moong, chana. Hero product to lead with: toor dal (highest household use).
NOTE: brand name use on packs/ads should wait for the 'SPICO' class 30 trademark check.

## Week 1 – Foundations (near-zero cost)
- Google Business Profile for BTM Layout premises (free).
- WhatsApp Business: catalogue of 4 pulses, price list, quick replies (free).
- Instagram page: 9 starter posts (product close-ups, "how we sort", recipes).
- Simple pricing sheet for kiranas and line suppliers (trade margin to come from Accounts).

## Week 2 – Kirana & line-supplier push (main channel while GS1 is on hold)
- Visit 10–15 kiranas per target area (Koramangala, Jayanagar, JP Nagar, Electronic City, MG Road).
- Leave-behind: A5 flyer + 250 g sample pack. Offer intro scheme (e.g. first-order extra margin or free 1 kg on 10 kg), amount set by Accounts/Cost Controller.
- Track visits/orders in a simple sheet.

## Week 3 – Households via apartments
- Apartment WhatsApp groups / MyGate & NoBrokerHood notice boards (often free or low cost) in target areas.
- "Try-pack" offer: 4 × 500 g combo at intro price, free delivery above a minimum order.
- Referral: ₹X off next order for each friend who orders.

## Week 4 – Review & quick-commerce prep
- Collect customer reviews/photos for Google & Instagram.
- Prepare Blinkit/Zepto/Instamart listing content (images, descriptions, nutrition panel once lab test is done).
- Keep what works, cut what doesn't.
