# Phased Roadmap

| Phase | Timing | Scope | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Pilot** | Weeks 1 to 3, used for first 2 to 3 months | Identity (Owner only), Catalog, Procurement (vendor, bill, receipt), Inventory (1 warehouse, ledger, batches), Production (repack batches, outsourced packer), Sales (customers, channels, GST invoice + PDF), Finance (payments, expenses, cash, numbering), Audit, Checklist, Dashboard, Excel import. Local, then free-tier hosting. | Tinku runs daily business on it for a month; stock matches physical count. |
| 2. Warehouse & Procurement depth | Months 3 to 5 | Multiple warehouses, bins, transfers, stock counts; purchase orders, vendor quotes and comparison; packaging material stock; barcode labels. | Second storage site live. |
| 3. Logistics/Transport | Months 5 to 8 | Vehicles, drivers, transporters, routes, trips, dispatch, proof of delivery (photo/OTP), freight cost allocation to invoices, e-way bill. | Deliveries planned and costed in-app. |
| 4. Staff & Controls | Months 6 to 9 (parallel) | Real users, roles/permissions UI, warehouse scoping, approvals (PO, discounts), audit viewer. | Staff use it without owner login. |
| 5. Finance/GST depth | Months 8 to 12 | GSTR-1/3B export, e-invoicing (IRN) when turnover requires, credit/debit notes, quick-commerce payout reconciliation, Tally export. | Monthly return prepared from the app. |
| 6. Scale & multi-company | Year 2+ | Second company/GSTIN, own printing and packing units with production planning, reporting warehouse/BI, mobile app or offline PWA, split heavy modules (e.g. Logistics) into services if load requires. | As business demands. |

The pilot is still small: only Phase 1 is built now. Later phases add tables/screens inside existing module boundaries; no rewrite.
